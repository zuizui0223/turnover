# Cover letter draft — Methods in Ecology and Evolution

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Known truth is not automatically assignable in simulation-based power studies,”** as a Research Article in *Methods in Ecology and Evolution*.

Simulation-based power studies commonly specify a target effect, generate data intended to contain that effect, and then evaluate recovery. Our manuscript shows that these steps can collapse three inferentially distinct events when the target is a constrained summary of simulated data: whether the target is structurally realizable on the realized design, whether the declared generator can actually assign that realizable target, and whether the estimator can recover it after successful assignment.

We do not claim that feasibility checks for constrained correlations, data-generating-mechanism effects on power, or lower power for discrete traits are new. The methodological contribution is the **separation of structural feasibility from generator-specific accessibility and downstream recovery**, together with prospective diagnostics for each gate.

We demonstrate the distinction using 722 real plant family × trait sampling geometries under one fixed phylogenetic-memory target while keeping observed trait values hidden. The key intervention is especially simple: all 276 categorical trees contain a realizable one-transition state at or above the benchmark, ruling out a hard one-transition ceiling below the target, yet the original latent-OU threshold generator fails to bracket the target in 79.7% of systems. Replacing only the generator with symmetric Mk2 reduces this failure to 61.6%, rescuing 51 original failures while creating one new failure. An exact factorization of binary state-balance attenuation identifies a second accessibility mechanism, and successful assignment remains distinct from downstream recovery.

We believe this is a Methods paper rather than a phylogenetic case study because the primary object is the inferential ordering of simulation-based power studies. The phylogenetic system serves as a stress test in which realizability, generator accessibility and recovery can be separated experimentally. The proposed audit applies whenever a target statistic is not itself a freely assignable parameter of the data-generating mechanism, including other discrete, bounded or otherwise constrained outcomes.

The mechanism programme was prospectively staged and closed under an unconditional stop rule before manuscript preparation. The submission includes an anonymized peer-review bundle containing the frozen design contracts, canonical results, source-backed figure generation and immutable workflow evidence; observed biological trait values are neither used nor required for the methodological results.
