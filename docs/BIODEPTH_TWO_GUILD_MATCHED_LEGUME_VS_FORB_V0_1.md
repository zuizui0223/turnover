# BIODEPTH 8-site matched two-guild replacement: grass+legume vs grass+forb v0.1

Frozen 2026-11-15, AFTER the exploratory 8-site functional-group count coefficients and 3/8 full-rank guild identity limitations were observed, but BEFORE opening this new source-specific **matched two-guild outcome**. It is an explicitly retrospective **post-result exploratory** biological contrast, not an independent confirmatory replication or relaxation of original 6-site exact-richness/20-plot feasibility HOLD.

## Fixed descriptive matched outcome estimand
- Source `Shoots.biomass` original dried standing aboveground herbaceous mass is the response; study protocols differ across sites (cut 5cm, once or twice yearly). Use `log1p(biomass)`, keep original legitimate zeros and drop only original NA/missing. Never call this annual NPP for all locations.
- Within each source site × richness × block × year stratum, if **both types have at least one valid response**: compute `D_{s,r,b,y} = mean(log1p(biomass) | grass+legume) − mean(log1p(biomass) | grass+forb)`. Each original species mix and plot contributes its harvested biomass only once per year; no abundance weighting or reuse of sown biomass as predictor.
- **Primary exploratory site estimate:** compute mean of `D` over 3 source years for each fixed site × richness × block stratum (with ≥1 year valid), then mean across eligible matched original strata at that site (each treatment comparison gets equal weight, irrespective of number of plot-year records). Record n strata and source year coverage; do not drop a site for a disagreeable outcome. If any site has no valid paired stratum, mark HOLD.
- Across 8 original sites, use the arithmetic unweighted mean of eight site means (site is independent geographic unit), with **10,000 site-resampling bootstrap replicates** seed **20261116**, report percentile 95% CI. A 95% interval including zero is unresolved; there is no significance search. Also report median and signs; source must have eight valid site estimates, otherwise do not claim eight-site mean.
- Prespecified secondary site-level regression comparator: `log1p(biomass) ~ 0+C(exact richness × original block)+C(year)+I(legume companion)` on *the same source-matched 110 plot* population by site. Inference must cluster original replicated plot-year rows by planted composition, with t degrees of freedom = (#source original composition clusters−1), and mark sites with <4 clusters or rank deficiency HOLD. The regression is not the primary equal-stratum estimand.
- Year-specific source descriptive strata mean (1,2,3) is a sensitivity only; never sum years as independent evidence or infer a trend because a single year is nominally significant.
- Source-conditional treatment reassignment can additionally permute **the whole legume-vs-forb labels only within exact original site × richness × block strata**, preserving original group-treatment counts; when replicates share species composition, permute composition-level assignments, not individual plot×year labels. A formal randomization p is not required and cannot be causal without original randomization documentation.

## Scientific boundaries
- This is a **planted guild substitution** proxy; individual species compositions also change, so cannot claim nitrogen fixation, traits/FD, competitive release or causal legume benefit.
- Published Spehn et al. 2005 and follow-ups already showed roles for legumes in biodiversity–functioning relationships: this is a *source-conditioned transportability diagnostic*, NOT an original result that legumes matter.
- The three guilds BIODEPTH (graminoids, N-fixing legumes, forbs) differ from Cedar E120's C3/C4 split and Woody group and Jena's small/tall herb split. The shared legume label does NOT ensure comparable plant species or harvest definitions. Do not meta-pool the coefficients as if same experimental interventions.
