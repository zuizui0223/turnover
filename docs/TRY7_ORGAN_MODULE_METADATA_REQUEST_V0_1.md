## Authorized PI request text (use https://www.try-db.org/TryWeb/Prop0.php)

**Proposed title:** Independently sourced plant reproductive–vegetative morphology for a preregistered cross-family phylogenetic-signal comparison

**Proposed project description:**

I am planning a held-out plant comparative analysis of whether reproductive
morphological traits (seed and fruit length, width and dry mass) tend to show
higher Blomberg phylogenetic signal than vegetative morphological traits
(leaf area, leaf length/width, petiole length, plant height and LMA) **when
compared using the same species and phylogenetic trees within each family**.

This hypothesis was generated by a previous AusTraits v7.0.0 analysis.
I am seeking **genuinely independently collected direct primary
measurements**, not the same original AusTraits data repackaged within TRY
or a newer compilation. The original study has already been exposed to
the exploratory organ-category hypothesis; data from it will not be counted
as prospectively independent validation.

Before requesting numerical measurements, would it be possible to receive
a **v7 metadata-only, non-phenotypic family×source×trait support report**
(or instructions to obtain one), covering the following frozen concepts?

- Seed length and width; **single-seed dry mass** distinguished from
  diaspores/whole fruits and fresh mass.
- Fruit length, width and dry mass distinguished from compound
  infructescences or samaras whose measured structure differs.
- Leaf area/width/length, petiole length, LMA and plant height,
  with explicit whole-leaf vs leaflet and petiole included/excluded
  metadata.
- Source `DatasetID`, title, original publication DOI and custodian;
  source-native specimen / ObservationID where available; collection
  protocol and whether the record is a *direct measurement* versus a
  flora/protologue/secondary compilation or imputed species mean.
- Taxon and family identifiers and counts of **distinct species with
  BOTH one reproductive and one vegetative trait from permitted
  independent original sources**, not merely pooled record counts.
- Original record or publication lineage links where available for
  excluding overlaps with the contributing AusTraits 7 source list,
  including Kew Seed Information Database and online floras.
- Data-release rights (public, registered IPG, custodian approval,
  embargo), with any restricted raw trait values excluded from this
  metadata-only stage.

Our frozen necessary biological support thresholds are at least **12
independent families**, **20 matched species per qualifying
family×reproductive×vegetative pair**, **six fruit-versus-vegetative and
six seed-versus-vegetative families**, and two distinct trait names per
organ category on **both** native phylogeny treatments. The thresholds
will not change in response to the data.

Could you advise whether a public source-level index or authorized
metadata-only TRY v7 request can assess these conditions, and how to
handle an independence audit against reused original records? I can use
the required PI registration, signed IPG and any data-owner permissions
before receiving protected observations. No redistribution of restricted
records is planned.

## Why ordinary public catalogues do not resolve this

- TRY v7 main database was released **2026-09-04** and the
  public Source File Archive listing is **not** a complete v7
  source-native trait support table.
- In the public File Archive's 122 entries, only three display both
  explicit leaf-size and seed/fruit-size field names. Of these,
  #10 is vegetation-plot means, #17 is 34 species, and #113 is
  209 Scleria species in just Cyperaceae. These are useful leads
  but no stand-alone 12-family validation.
- Scleria source Zenodo v1 `10.5281/zenodo.10533114` is visible
  as open, whereas latest v2 `10.5281/zenodo.12755860`
  restricts its raw files. Do not request v2 without legitimate
  permissions or assume v1 usage rights extend to v2.
- The provider's official request page requires PI registration and
  IPG acceptance. Automated assistant access cannot impersonate the
  PI or sign this agreement.
