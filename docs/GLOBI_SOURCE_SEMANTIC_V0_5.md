# GloBI source-semantic gate v0.5

## Decision

**HOLD — primary joint interaction route stops before any turnover outcome is opened.**

The GloBI route passed archive identity, schema, aggregate structural support, and a stricter within-programme structural recheck. It nevertheless fails the pre-frozen semantic/provenance requirement for the primary rewiring analysis.

## Gate chronology

1. Stable GloBI Zenodo record 0.11: PASS.
2. Archive schema / aliases: PASS.
3. Structural support v0.4: PASS — 815 candidate systems, 92 structurally qualifying systems, 62 sourceNamespace clusters.
4. Correct-grain support v0.5.2: PASS — 58 provenance-resolved programme blocks in 20 namespaces independently satisfy the frozen 20-focal / 5-site-year / 10-repeated-focal thresholds.
5. Source-semantic gate v0.5: HOLD.

## Why the semantic gate fails

The frozen gate requires at least 12 independent source programmes that are genuine replicated ecological interaction surveys with reconstructable network units and acceptable sampling-effort semantics.

Nine of the 20 remaining namespaces are definitively ineligible without inspecting any turnover outcome:

- natural-history / specimen / occurrence aggregators;
- a citizen-science interaction aggregation;
- an IPT occurrence archive;
- the GloBI adapter of Mangal, which cannot count as independent evidence after Mangal already failed as the first primary source.

That leaves an absolute upper bound of 11 potentially eligible namespaces. Since the frozen requirement is 12, the gate is mathematically unreachable. The remaining 11 uncertain candidates do not need to be forced into PASS or HOLD categories.

## Consequence

The programme does **not**:

- lower the threshold;
- count mirrors or adapters as independent programmes;
- treat occurrence/specimen coordinates as replicated networks;
- add a third primary interaction source;
- open rewiring, distance-dissimilarity, phylogenetic, or time-space coupling outcomes.

The interaction arm therefore remains a prospective/source-qualification boundary result rather than generalized interaction-turnover evidence.

## Scientific implication for the programme

This HOLD does not invalidate the time-space turnover question. It shows that globally integrated interaction archives can look highly replicated at the record level while failing the stronger requirement needed for comparative rewiring: independent ecological survey units with interpretable opportunity and effort.

The trait arm can proceed independently under its already-frozen source order.
