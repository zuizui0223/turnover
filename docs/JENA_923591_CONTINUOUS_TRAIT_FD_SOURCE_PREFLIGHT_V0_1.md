# Continuous-trait FD availability for the Jena experiment — source gate v0.1 (2026-11-14)

## Reason to open a *new* provenance investigation
The separate 2003 Jena direct harvested biomass pilot has been completed and archived without post-hoc adjustment: 82 independently randomized plots. May functional-group-richness coefficient on log1p dry standing biomass (with log2 sown species richness and block fixed effects) is +0.16789 (HC3 p=0.0439, within-block 2000-plot-bootstrap 95% interval +0.0219 to +0.3521). August coefficient +0.00168 (p=0.9829), hold-one-block-out SSE gain +0.007856, additional legume-presence adjustment p=0.2214. This is a season-dependent, low-transportability within-site *category-level* result, not true continuous FD, phylogenetic memory or macroecology.

## Fixed external source candidate (investigate availability, not effects)
van der Plas et al. (2024), PANGAEA DOI [10.1594/PANGAEA.923591](https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.923591), metadata advertises 59 of the 60 planted species with **41 plant traits**, plus species composition and 42 ecosystem functions measured during 2003–2012 (78 mixture plots). Companion article van der Plas et al. (2020), *Plant traits alone are poor predictors of ecosystem properties and long-term ecosystem functioning*, Nature Ecology & Evolution DOI [10.1038/s41559-020-01316-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-020-01316-9). The dataset publisher explicitly notes the usual final author approval was not obtained (https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.923591).

**Source-overlap warning:** This is the SAME Jena experiment and overlapping 2003 plant community measurements, not biological validation of the primary May 2003 effect. Traits are compiled across original literature, pots and monocultures; they are not confirmed independent of the Jena dry-mass responses and may contain imputed values.

## Tier 0 permitted only: inspect catalog, downloadable file list, checksums and schemas
1. Content-negotiate original DOI using `Accept: text/tab-separated-values`. Validate DOI and 2024 release metadata. PANGAEA landing page says there are **7 binary attachment entries**, not 7 independent empirical samples; avoid mistaking secondary model R² files for original species trait measurements.
2. Identify original trait table file names, format, raw/estimated/imputed annotation, number of trait columns and species, source-references. Extract **only file names, byte sizes, hashes, header fields, row counts**, not raw trait values or trait–biomass effect coefficients. All source objects remain temporary, no copyright-restricted original data added to Git.
3. Exact species binomials must link independently to at least 57 of the 59 independently sourced trait taxa AND the 60 Jena sown species; absence of source origin/units or ambiguous traits fails source gate. These proposed thresholds are for a separate exploratory Jena trait-FD extension, not retroactive modification of the older 90%-exact/12-family global primary programme.
4. If a valid table exists, *before any trait-based effect opening*, freeze the trait group definitions, standardization, missing-data rules, FD target (e.g., multi-axis Rao Q vs FRic or FDis), plot coverage threshold, spatial/temporal split and block handling. The separate 2003 pilot already opens May and August outcomes, so every new trait-FD test is **post-2003 exploratory** unless evaluated on a held-out future year and its source selected independently of association outcomes.
5. The 2020 NE&E article already reports limited predictive value of plant traits for long-term ecosystem properties; a new analysis must not claim novelty merely for testing whether more traits predict biomass.

## Hard stop
The global cross-family trait-memory/PD–FD plan in PR #39 remains PRE_OUTCOME HOLD (87.7% vs 90% strict exact names; 10 vs 12 BIEN families). No new source may be added as an outcome-dependent rescue of that frozen programme. Quantitative FD, biomass effect, seasonal mechanisms, or phylogenetic memory must NOT be estimated in this Tier 0 probe.
