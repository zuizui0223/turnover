# Separate new independent harvested-biomass/leaf-trait source pilot: Utrecht BioCliVE 2026

**Added 2026-11-18 as a clearly labelled NEW independent source candidate after Zenodo Ali 19609795 PLOT_SUMMARY_ONLY_HOLD**, before any original BioCliVE file contents or ecological associations were opened. This is **not** a reset of the closed BIEN/sPlotOpen global source family, and no data source is selected by a desired positive effect.

## Verified published primary data intent

- **Pan, de Bello et al. (2026)**, `Relating functional traits within and across species to the stabilization of grassland productivity`, *Journal of Ecology* DOI `10.1111/1365-2745.70306`. **Original public Zenodo DOI** `10.5281/zenodo.18483939` / `https://zenodo.org/api/records/18483939`.
- The original paper states a **single Utrecht UU BioCliVE experimental programme**, two blocks, **88 total source experimental plots**, sampled **2018–2022**. At peak season each year, species-level aboveground biomass was clipped above 8cm, sorted to species, dried at 70°C and weighed. Original SLA/LDMC leaf measurements were made in **July 2022 only**, >2000 original leaf samples. **Trait measurements from 2022 should not be represented as the actual 2018–2021 species-specific phenotype**.
- The authors themselves tested within-/interspecific trait differences and species stability/asynchrony, so an output using this same published dataset is **not independent replication of the authors' published positive/negative results**. A new analysis requires an explicitly different frozen estimand.
- Original source contains only roughly **10 analysed plant species** (after original exclusions in authors' analysis), so **cannot** internally estimate originally qualified family-specific Blomberg K across 12 families×20 taxa. At best it could later be an **independent experimental test of the difference between intraspecific trait variation, species means and biomass fluctuations**, not a global K/FD/AGB bridge.
