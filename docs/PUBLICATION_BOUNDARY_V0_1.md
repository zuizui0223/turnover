# Publication boundary — temporal trait-memory study

Status: publication-scope record after completion of the BIEN prospective analyses and before completion of the independent AusTraits replication.

## Main empirical paper

The completed paper is a **temporal comparative plant-trait study** based on the prospectively qualified BIEN core:

- 201 family × trait systems;
- 45 vascular-plant families;
- 12 continuous traits;
- memory gradient = Spearman association between patristic separation and trait-state dissimilarity;
- S3 primary and backbone-native prune-only sensitivity.

The paper's empirical core is complete. It does not depend on completion of the AusTraits replication.

## Primary result hierarchy

### 1. Cross-lineage portability

Trait identity does not robustly improve prediction of memory strength in a completely held-out family.

- S3 gain = -0.024, p = 0.091.
- prune-only gain = -0.032, p = 0.193.

This is the clearest primary claim because it directly tests the transfer question.

### 2. Crossed repeatability

On the same fixed core:

- family repeatability share = 0.150 (95% bootstrap CI 0.020–0.286);
- trait repeatability share = 0.043 (0–0.134);
- unresolved system-level/residual share = 0.806 (0.660–0.952).

These components are descriptive repeatability shares. The prospectively frozen family-vs-trait variance-ratio comparison failed its pre-outcome precision gate and must not be reconstructed from the estimates.

### 3. Conditional family context

After trait-specific means are removed:

- conditional family repeatability = 0.183;
- 95% bootstrap CI = 0.029–0.337;
- prune-only = 0.168;
- leave-one-out range = 0.121–0.227.

The interval spans the predeclared 0.10 practical reference. Report the estimated component and uncertainty; do not claim that family repeatability confidently exceeds 10%.

## Complementary result

A post-outcome exploratory test finds no detectable smooth organization of the recurring family context across deeper family phylogeny:

- S3 rho = -0.015, p = 0.822;
- prune-only rho = -0.011, p = 0.871;
- mean-score rho = -0.009, p = 0.887;
- leave-one-trait-out range = -0.038 to +0.017.

This supports describing the observed lineage context as a **family-level mosaic at this resolution**, rather than a continuously inherited deep-family regime. It is complementary, not a prospective primary result.

## Main biological statement

> A plant trait can show a real phylogenetic memory gradient within a family without carrying a portable memory strength to another family. The useful unit of generalization is therefore closer to a trait-in-lineage context than to the trait label alone.

The modest recurring family component provides supporting evidence that lineage context matters, but most variation remains unresolved at the individual family × trait system level.

## What is not part of the empirical result

The following belong in Methods/Supplement, audit trail, or future work rather than the biological Results narrative:

- failure of the original interaction arm;
- failure of the original spatial trait arm;
- Mangal/GloBI source qualification history;
- AusTraits failure under the old joint time-space gate;
- failed family-vs-trait variance-ratio precision gate;
- failed backbone-stability supporting gate;
- failed family-context leave-one-trait-out portability power gate.

These failures are valuable evidence of prospective discipline, not biological null results.

## Manuscript framing

Preferred title:

**Cross-lineage prediction reveals limited portability of phylogenetic trait memory**

Conceptual alternative:

**Phylogenetic trait memory is context-dependent across plant families**

Avoid:
- “lineages dominate traits”;
- “phylogenetic signal is not a trait property”;
- “family effects are stronger than trait effects”;
- “memory decays at different rates” unless a true time/rate parameter is modeled.

## Final provenance robustness and analysis stop

A post-outcome sensitivity audited the strongest remaining observational alternative: family-correlated BIEN source composition. Family context was unrelated to source HHI (rho = -0.045, p = 0.773), dominant-source share (rho = -0.066, p = 0.666), citation HHI (rho = -0.061, p = 0.693), or dominant-citation share (rho = -0.062, p = 0.685). R_family changed only from 0.1504 unadjusted to 0.1492 after source adjustment and 0.1470 after source-citation adjustment.

This sensitivity was post-outcome and does not establish a causal biological family effect. It does, however, weaken the specific explanation that the recurring family component is a simple consequence of family differences in BIEN provenance concentration.

Under the pre-frozen stop rule, the BIEN empirical analyses are now **closed**. No additional provenance-field search, covariate mining, outcome-driven filtering, or new primary hypothesis is permitted on the 201 observed effects.

## Submission readiness

The BIEN empirical analysis is scientifically closed. Remaining work for the main paper is presentation, literature positioning, figure production, and ordinary reproducibility checks—not additional hypothesis hunting on the 201 observed effects.
