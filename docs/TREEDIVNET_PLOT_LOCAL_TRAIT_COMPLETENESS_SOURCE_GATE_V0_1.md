# TreeDivNet 2026 measured-in-plot SLA+LNCM → DS1 biomass: original source *completeness* gate

**Fixed 2026-11-17 before opening any new original biomasses or SLA/LNCM effects.** This branch is a NEW, exploratory estimand proposed after the original Jensen et al. 2026 authors' positive woody/FDis/mixing findings and the independent turnover original Table S1 year-source eligibility analyses. It is **not independent confirmation** of published authors' results and **not** the original separately stricter individual-tree/source-scale qualification from PR #55/56, which remains HOLD.

## Scientific estimand separation

The original authors separately publish `DS1.xlsx` (one unique `site×block×plot` woody standing stock Mg dry matter ha−1) and `DS2.xlsx` (species×plot SLA, LNCM etc.); Methods S3 of the exact uploaded 15-page supplement says leaf nutrient concentrations were summarized **by original species and plot**. The original 2026 article methods §2.4 uses observed plot–species mean leaf values and §2.5 uses a different monoculture/reference/TRY-filled FDis table. Proposed NEW predictive question, **not yet estimated**: does *in situ species-trait dispersion* add out-of-experiment woody biomass prediction beyond planted species richness and within-plot mean SLA/N? It explicitly does NOT try to reconstruct individual harvested tree mass from DS2 species woody and therefore does not use the unresolved `mean(DS2 species woody)=DS1 plot mass` 321/328 normalization. The earlier source provenance HOLD still forbids declaring an individual-tree measurement/cause.

## Prespecified stricter *source-only* plot trait completeness

Before any FD or biomass relationship:
1. Require original `site × block × plot` keys present and unique in DS1, DS2 species rows unique by species; original `species_richness`, treatment and species_composition agree.
2. Require original **DS1 planted richness exactly equals the number of unique DS2 source species rows** for that plot. Do not impute, replace missing species or alter source recorded richness. Six known original total plot-richness mismatches are potential exclusions; report whether they appear in the prior 117 source cohort.
3. Require **≥2 positive finite SLA AND LNCM on the SAME two or more DS2 source species records**, both original study-inventory years within ≤2 calendar years of woody stock, excluding ECOLINK.
4. Require full-two-trait measured source species coverage **≥80% of all species named in the original DS2 plant composition/source richness** for each original plot (`n_complete_joint_SLA_LNCM / original_DS1_species_richness >=0.8`). Without complete joint traits for most planted species, the FD geometry would not represent the entire stand.
5. New source eligibility structural PASS if at least **80** original independent plots, **≥5** named original experimental sites each with **≥8** eligible source plots and **≥20** different source species with full SLA+LNCM coverage. Fixed thresholds inherited from the prior source program. Report all cohorts and site counts on 117 source plots **before** imposing this stricter completeness and 107 sensitivity.
6. Export **only source counts** (site-level experiment names allowed as original published public experiment identifiers), taxon support counts, trait missingness/coverage bins, original plot source richness/DS2 row count mismatch summary, and eligibility status. No named source plot IDs, sampled species names, original SLA/LNCM or biomass numeric values persisted. No ecological FD, PD, K, or biomass coefficient computed in this phase.
7. If the stricter source support **fails**, status `PLOT_LOCAL_FD_SOURCE_COMPLETENESS_HOLD`; **do not relax** the 80% threshold or assert a new biological effect from this population. If it passes, a separate **new outcome-frozen, held-out-experiment** prediction design may be authored *without changing the historical strict original individual-provenance gate*, as an explicitly exploratory plot-level prediction (not causal or individual source measurement replication).

## Limitations

- Original observed species traits may be **responses to mixture and growth**, so a later predictive association is endogenous: cannot infer that trait dispersion *caused* biomass. Authors' own positive FDis–NDE result comes from monoculture/TRY trait reference values, a different estimand from here.
- Tree wood biomass is **site-specific allometry-derived**, not independently destructive direct biomass. MataDIV wood-density WD is used in its biomass equations, so WD is deliberately excluded from this proposed source predictor; only SLA and LNCM considered.
- No original per-tree sample IDs and dates in DS1/DS2; published original Methods S3 and Table S1 provide original species×plot aggregation protocol and study-variable inventory years, but not individual records. New study conclusions may concern published aggregates only.
- Seven original study sites supply the 117 plot-local source upper bound, with **MyDiv 58/117** plot concentration; site-equal out-of-source-site evaluation, not treating plots as 117 independent ecosystems, is essential.
- **No family-level Blomberg K or BIEN rho is estimated**; original 40 study species are insufficient for 12 independent families×20 species source criterion, and no global BIEN/sPlotOpen, original BIODEPTH or NEON source hold is reopened.
