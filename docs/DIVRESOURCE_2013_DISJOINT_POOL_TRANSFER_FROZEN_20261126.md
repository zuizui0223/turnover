# DivResource 2013: cross-species-pool transfer of assigned functional dissimilarity

2026-11-26. New original field programme (Bad Lauchstädt, not Jena TBE, Cedar Creek or Cadotte). Siebenkäs, Schumacher & Roscher (2016), PLOS ONE, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0158110. The 96-plot field experiment contains two disjoint pools of four species, each the full grass/forb × short/tall design. Eight blocks; shade assigned at block level; fertilization varies within blocks. The two-species compositions span all six possible pairs per pool and four resource treatments. Original source S5 (2013 annual sown biomass, sum of May and August), S6 (published diversity components); original tables under article Supporting Information. Source S7 uses outcome-year species biomass weights and is NOT used as a predictor.

## Fixed question before inspecting data values
Can known plant functional differences transfer across the two disjoint species pools, after controlling resource condition and mean functional identity? This is a **within-field across-pool transport test**, not independent field replications. Use only 2-species mixtures (expected 48), equal originally sown viable seed proportions (1/2 each), matched species monocultures in each exact fertilizer×shade condition and species pool. Four-species mixtures excluded: only one composition per pool. No use of observed biomass proportions to define FD.

M0: intercept + 3 resource-condition indicators + sown grass fraction + sown tall fraction. M1 adds fixed 2-axis categorical dissimilarity D = (different functional group + different stature)/2. Train one whole species pool, test the other, and reverse. The assignment taxonomy and all source IDs must be verified from original published design and tables, not inferred from observed abundances. Same source rows and folds for absolute annual sown biomass B and NDE = B − (M1_species+M2_species)/2. Compare pool-equal heldout SSE gain G = 1 − SSE_add/SSE_base and G_NDE − G_B. Report both directions separately, no significance testing or population CI with only two pools. Same exact resource-treatment and single-species monoculture context required; one physical monoculture per species/treatment may limit baseline uncertainty. If complete exact source assignment, 48 pair/treatment records or full-rank designs cannot be verified, STOP. This source-specific categorical design is deliberately distinct from continuous SLA FD and original BIEN K/rho.

No change to earlier PRs or original evidence: the published paper's already reported fertilization and diversity results are not new discoveries. No K→FD causal inference.
