## Aim and unit mismatch

A central question raised by the positive review is whether *the same pre-mixing functional disparity* predicts a **mixture's net diversity gain** more consistently across whole experiments than it predicts **absolute stand productivity**. The same original mixture records, original trait definition and source experiment IDs are mandatory on both outcomes. `FD→NDE` is not a proven causal mediator merely because mixture assignment was experimental. Outcome, trait ascertainment, study age and composition may be confounded.

## Causal interpretation and hard firewall

The existing Turner/BIEN phylogenetic rho and AusTraits K cannot be used to back-fill causal family×trait→FD→biomass mediation: these datasets do not independently support the original ≥12 families×20 species biological K design. Distinguish: (i) experimental random assignment of planted *species combinations*, (ii) non-random numerical FD attached to combinations, (iii) observed realised FD after competition, and (iv) net diversity effects calculated from monoculture baselines. Never declare a simple observational FD→NDE coefficient a randomized FD intervention. Never re-run formerly failed old source combinations to find favourable trait/response/region splits.
