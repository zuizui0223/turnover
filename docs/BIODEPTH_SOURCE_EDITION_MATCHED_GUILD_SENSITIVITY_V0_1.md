## Exact response and fair common-cohort comparison
- For each original site×richness×block×year matched treatment stratum, keep only plot-year rows with **both 2005 Shoots and 2010 Community original responses numeric and nonnegative**, retaining real 0 and not filling missing. This yields a *common source-complete-case cohort* and identical treatment strata for two alternative response editions.
- Count source-complete-case original plot-years; require ≥95 source-matched stratum-year comparisons across ≥30 of original 35 strata, all eight sites. Otherwise HOLD.
- For each common matched stratum-year, `D_{source}=mean(log1p(biomass)|grass+legume)−mean(log1p(biomass)|grass+forb)`; average years within an exact original stratum; average strata within each original site; then mean all eight sites equally. Different source versions require **separate** D values computed for the **same** original plot-year rows.
- Report each site's 2005 and 2010 D, their 2010−2005 difference and whether its sign changes, 8-site mean for each source, and 10,000 site-cluster bootstrap CI for source difference, seed **20261115**. This is not a newly independently sampled eight-site population. The 2005 full-source historical n327 mean +0.17147 is **not** directly the common-source matched 2005 mean if the common-case cohort is smaller; report both separately.
- Report 2010 original Community versus 2005 Shoots source numeric quality, and do not select an edition based on more favorable p. No direct ecological causal or trait-FD model; source-edition effects are dataset processing/definition sensitivity, not biological gains.
