## Fixed design contrasts
For each pool and each F,S treatment context, the four functional types yield six pairs: two with only stature difference (H-only), two with only grass/forb difference (G-only), two with both (HG). Each category partitions all four species into two nonoverlapping pairs. Compare two mean yields per category:
C_H = mean(HG) − mean(G-only); C_G = mean(HG) − mean(H-only).
Each mean includes each species once; any additive species-incidence expectation cancels exactly across all three matchings, separately by resource context. Thus additive species productivity cannot explain these contrasts, and C(B)=C(NDE); the two contrast axes are correlated and are not independent observations. This is a nonadditive pair-composition contrast, not a randomized scalar FD-only effect. The original D=(G+H)/2 pooled the axes; this is a separate analysis rather than choosing another FD metric based on its score.

Recompute original species annual yields from S5 with the same-context positive singleton monocultures and equal sown proportions. B=sum(Y), E=mean(M), NDE=B−E; RYT=sum(Y/M); CE=(RYT−1)E; SE=B−RYT*E; DE=B/RYT−E; TDCE=SE−DE. Require the parent original 64 CE/SE/DE/TDCE printed-precision identity checks and replay of previous frozen source results. Use internally coherent reconstructed yields in the new contrasts (published rounded S6 used only for source validation). Verify all six pair assignments and balanced categories in every cell and exact C(E)≈0 and C(B)≈C(NDE). E is not a scored response because it cancels by construction.

## Fixed two-direction prediction comparison
For each of B,NDE,CE,SE,TDCE,DE, predict the two contrasts at all four resource contexts of the other species pool (8 correlated contrast values per heldout pool, 2 pools). M0: separate constant mean contrast per axis, estimated from train pool. Mmatch: C_H = intercept + shade, C_G = intercept + fertilizer. Mcross: C_H = intercept + fertilizer, C_G = intercept + shade. Mfull (fixed descriptive secondary model): each contrast = intercept + shade + fertilizer (no F×S). No resource-specific trait means, no outcome-weighted FD, no model selection. Report all three increments vs M0 using raw g dry mass/m² SSE across axes and resource contexts; same heldout data for every model. Also report axis-specific pooled gains, both transfer directions and all component slopes; matched-model criterion per endpoint: positive gain in BOTH directions, pooled superiority over Mcross, positive pooled gain in BOTH axes. No CI/p-values; only two pools in one field, contrast observations not independent. Baseline zero SSE is a declared undefined score, not an opportunity to drop an endpoint. B and NDE are an identity check, not two independent tests. 

## Directional relative-yield diagnostic
Among the four different-stature pairs per pool/context, compute mean [relative yield(tall)−relative yield(short)], where each species mixture yield is divided by its own matched same-treatment monoculture. Similarly among the four grass/forb pairs compute mean [relative yield(grass)−relative yield(forb)]. Each species in the positive or negative class occurs twice. For each pool, report both F and S differences averaged over the other factor for each directional index, all signs including reversals. These are descriptive normalized yield allocation, not direct competition or uptake measurements. Do not claim predicted sign unless replicated; test no additional trait axes after outcomes.

The positive/negative model comparisons and directional indices are exploratory after prior analyses; no independent biological replication from the same site. No K→FD causal mediation, no underlying individual source observations exported. Report source and result status, retain main and prior PRs unchanged.
