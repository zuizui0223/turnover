# Trait-memory robustness interpretation contract v0.3

Frozen before opening v0.3 robustness outcomes.

## Original claims under audit

The original temporal results support two descriptive statements:

- naive crossed-lmer variance shares: family ≈0.15, trait ≈0.04, residual ≈0.81;
- unshrunk leave-one-family-out trait-mean portability gain is negative on both S3 and prune-only trees.

These results remain historical facts. This robustness programme decides how strongly they can be interpreted.

## A. Sampling-error-aware repeatability

The primary quantity is the crossed meta-analytic heterogeneity decomposition after adding species-bootstrap sampling variances.

### If sampling variance explains a large share

If the typical sampling-variance share is material and the system-level heterogeneity share is no longer clearly the largest heterogeneity component, the manuscript must **drop** language that “most variation is family-by-trait specificity” or “most memory is system-specific.”

Allowed replacement:

> Much of the apparent cell-level variance in raw rho combines finite-species estimation error with residual between-system heterogeneity.

### If system heterogeneity remains dominant

A stronger system-specific statement is allowed only if the measurement-aware system heterogeneity share is the largest component and the pre-frozen cluster-bootstrap uncertainty supports that ordering.

Even then, call it **unexplained between-system heterogeneity**, not a biological mechanism.

## B. Family repeatability versus correlated-trait artifact

The conservative correlated-trait null preserves the predeclared redundant-trait dependence blocks and the exact family × trait incidence while setting the family main effect to zero.

### If observed R_family is inside the null

Do not interpret R_family as a lineage-level “memory regime” or family context.

Allowed statement:

> The apparent family repeatability is compatible with correlated/redundant trait outcomes on a shared sparse family design.

### If observed R_family exceeds the null

The family component survives this specific redundancy/shared-design explanation.

Allowed statement:

> Family repeatability exceeds the level generated by the predeclared correlated-trait outcome structure under a zero-family-effect null.

This still does **not** identify a biological mechanism.

## C. Raw versus log trait scale

### If raw and log rho agree closely and model conclusions are stable

The temporal result is robust to multiplicative trait scale.

### If log transformation materially changes rho or repeatability/portability conclusions

The manuscript must call the raw-scale result **scale-dependent** and cannot present the raw dissimilarity metric as a general trait-memory measure.

No trait-specific transform selection is allowed after seeing the result.

## D. Portability with shrinkage

The original unshrunk portability HOLD is never converted into a positive result by this secondary analysis.

### If BLUP gain remains nonpositive

The negative/zero portability conclusion is robust to shrinkage.

### If BLUP gain becomes slightly positive but training trait variance is near zero

Do **not** say a stable trait signature becomes portable.

Preferred interpretation:

> Unshrunk trait means were noisy, but the shrinkage model finds little transferable trait-level variation to borrow.

### If BLUP gain is clearly positive and trait variance is non-negligible

Say that the original arithmetic-mean predictor was inefficient; portability evidence becomes method-sensitive. The frozen v0.2 HOLD remains historical and is not relabelled.

## E. Ecological scope

No outcome in this robustness programme can establish:
- spatial turnover;
- the original time × space generality question;
- a new ecological mechanism;
- adaptation, niche conservatism, or family-specific evolutionary regimes.

The maximum biological conclusion remains about **how safely phylogenetic-memory estimates can be borrowed across traits or lineages**.

## Publication decision

The temporal paper remains publishable as a methods-aware empirical caution if:
- reference identity passes;
- measurement-aware decomposition is estimable;
- raw/log scale sensitivity is reported honestly;
- family repeatability is interpreted according to the correlated-trait null;
- portability is phrased according to the shrinkage result.

If the family component disappears under the null and most residual variance is sampling error, the paper must be reframed around **limited transferable information in noisy phylogenetic-signal estimates**, not “lineage-specific memory regimes.”


## Observed v0.3 decision

The frozen robustness programme is now complete.

### Sampling-error-aware repeatability

All 201 raw effects were reproduced at the archived four-decimal precision, and every system received a valid species-cluster bootstrap SE.

For S3, the measurement-aware point decomposition using the mean sampling variance gives approximately:

- family: 15.1% of typical total variance;
- trait: 5.7%;
- residual between-system heterogeneity: 54.9%;
- sampling error: 24.2%.

For prune-only the corresponding point shares are approximately 14.0%, 2.2%, 58.6%, and 25.3%.

The S3 two-way family × trait cluster bootstrap completed 500/500 refits. Its heterogeneity-share intervals are broad: family 0.123–0.749, trait approximately 0–0.385, and system 0.071–0.751.

**Decision:** the original residual share of 0.8063 must not be called biological system specificity. System heterogeneity is the largest point component, but it is not demonstrably dominant over the family component under cluster-bootstrap uncertainty.

### Correlated-trait/shared-design artifact

The observed family repeatability exceeds the frozen zero-family-effect correlated-trait null on both tree treatments:

- S3: R_family = 0.1504, null 95% interval 0–0.1385, p = 0.017;
- prune-only: R_family = 0.1482, null 95% interval 0–0.1217, p = 0.009.

Collapsing the 12 trait labels into five predeclared trait domains leaves R_family at 0.144 (S3) and 0.147 (prune-only).

**Decision:** family repeatability survives this specific redundant-trait/shared-incidence explanation. It may be described as repeatable lineage context, but not as a known biological mechanism.

### Shrinkage-aware portability

The original unshrunk gains were −0.0242 (S3) and −0.0324 (prune-only).

Training-only BLUP shrinkage changes them to +0.0060 and −0.0048, respectively.

**Decision:** drop language suggesting that trait identity is strongly anti-portable. The supported conclusion is that transferable trait-level signal is weak: shrinkage removes most of the prediction penalty but does not produce robust positive portability across both tree treatments.

### Log scale

The frozen all-201 natural-log sensitivity is unavailable because 25 S3 systems and 18 prune-only systems contain at least one nonpositive species median.

**Decision:** retain this as a real limitation. Do not add offsets, signed logs, or post-outcome system filtering inside v0.3.

## Final wording boundary

Allowed:

> Phylogenetic trait memory contains a repeatable family-level component that is not explained by the predeclared correlated-trait null, but trait identity carries little robust information that transfers to an unseen family.

Allowed:

> A substantial share of the old cell-level residual is finite-species estimation error, and the remaining between-system heterogeneity is too uncertain to call dominant.

Not allowed:

- “Most phylogenetic memory is system-specific.”
- “Trait identity is anti-portable.”
- “Family context is the dominant determinant.”
- “The family component identifies a lineage-specific evolutionary mechanism.”
- “The raw-scale result is invariant to multiplicative trait scaling.”
