## Fixed source cohort, determined before accessing any biological response values

Single target: **August 2021** original main BioCON M 4-/9-species mixtures only. Whole-plot CO2 assignment ring (six rings), fixed N, original planted species identities and treatment, no additional watering/warming (original Water trt=0 and Temp trt=0). Exclude bare, monocultures from model rows, supplemental S plots, and 16-species mixture group: every 16-species plot has same originally sown combination, so leave-one-planted-composition-out is not identifiable within 16-species richness. Do not convert June missing 2021 biomass to zero. Each eligible main mixture must have original nonmissing August 2021 AbovegroundTotal Biomass (g/m²), all constituent sown species with two independently planted original monoculture plot biomasses in exact August 2021 CO2×N context, and ≥80% source candidate native-E141 SLA coverage with ≥2 covered sown species. The source-only screen previously passed **46 physical plots: 36 richness-4, 10 richness-9**, 46 unique planted-composition groups, six rings; only an exploratory same-field prediction, not 46 independent studies.

Published original 1997 equal seed **mass** of 12 g m⁻² divided evenly among sown species fixes p_i=1/S. For original source total AbovegroundTotal Biomass (g/m²) B and two original independent monoculture totals (same year, month, CO2, N) mean M_i, define E=Σ_i M_i/S and signed NDE=B−E. Do not treat E as a species composition intervention, do not confuse signed NDE with an observed/expected *ratio* R_mix, and do not log-transform signed NDE.

## Frozen models and crossvalidation

- Treatment-specific log(SLA) per author processed original 14 E141 species (numeric must be finite >0); only the subset of sown species in each community that have valid direct-source-candidate SLA values contributes to CWM and candidate FD. The original population is **not** a complete 16-species trait dataset; no imputation or renaming after outcome access.
- CWM = mean log(SLA) among observed candidate species under plot's actual CO2×N treatment.
- **One-dimensional Rao-type FD** = Σ_{i,j} |log(SLA_i)−log(SLA_j)| / m² for m directly covered source species. This is an equal-weighted subset dissimilarity and cannot be marketed as full multidimensional FDis.
- M0 predictors: intercept, 9-species richness indicator, N enrichment indicator, five original Ring fixed-effect dummies (which **absorb original CO2**; never add aliased CO2 dummy), and the CWM. M1 adds that single FD variable. Train-fold-only predictor centering/scaling of CWM and FD; no interactions or other trait/transform shopping.
- Leave-one-*original sown 16-species composition vector*-out across all plots, N and rings; group all matching assignments, not random 2021 records. Equal-ring weighted least squares in each training fold; every held-out fold must be full-rank M0 and M1, and every training source ring represented. No covariate dropping after outcome inspection.
- Separate outcomes on the **same exact fixed plots/folds**: untransformed original August 2021 absolute B and signed NDE (g m⁻²). For each endpoint define ring-equal original out-of-fold incremental SSE gain G=1−SSE(M1)/SSE(M0). Primary reported comparison is G_NDE−G_B in **percentage points**; do not choose best endpoint after fitting.
- For descriptive uncertainty only, 2000 preseeded original CO2-stratified (3 ambient+3 enriched) **ring-cluster resamples of fixed out-of-fold residuals**, no refits; six whole rings make CI weak. Additionally compare signed NDE out-of-fold gains under each of the **two** same-context original monoculture replicates alone, selected by deterministic original physical plot key, as a fixed sensitivity to E uncertainty. This does not constitute full sampling-error propagation or a causal estimate.
- No outcome data, numeric biomass, or fitted effects have been inspected before writing this frozen analysis contract. No forward prediction, no p-value mining, no cross-site meta-analysis.
